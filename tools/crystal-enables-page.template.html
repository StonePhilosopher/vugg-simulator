Read left to right. A species can only appear once something in the column before it is already there — either standing in the vug as a substrate, or dissolving and handing over its ingredients. Five species start the whole cascade, because they need no other crystal at all.

What an arrow means. It is a gate condition in the nucleation engine, not a guess about paragenesis. A solid wire means the source must be present (substrate) or dissolving (replacement) before the target can nucleate. Dashed violet means the two enable each other — five real mutual pairs exist, and they are drawn as they are rather than cut to force a tidy chart.

Columns are dependency depth, computed as the longest path from a root. A species sits in column n because the deepest chain reaching it is n steps long — so a column is a floor, not a schedule. Nothing here says two species in one column grow at the same time.

The 28 species not shown take part in no crystal-to-crystal gate at all: they answer to broth chemistry, temperature and redox alone. Absence from this chart is not absence from the simulator.
