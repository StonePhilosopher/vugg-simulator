## Scientific decision actually made

At the authored MVT endpoint, booked calcite is about 5,425.56 mm³; its full Wulff
body is about 1,363.92 mm³; attachment leaves about 813.67 mm³, with no contact
loss. This is substantial disagreement even before clipping. Source dimensions
are the mass model's ellipsoid-equivalent extents, while the Wulff envelope uses
its own normalized shape.

The user explicitly chose: **preserve mass rules; record factors; hold
production**. This tranche therefore records per-specimen representation factors
and both volumes. It does not relabel the discrepancy as porosity, rescale a
mesh, alter density, reduce booked inventory, or recalibrate growth. The
relationship is arithmetic closure, not physical-solid closure.

The book remains chemistry/fill/occupancy/inventory-return authority. Completed
surfaces are reconstruction and surface-inspection data only. Both quantities
must remain named in future reports. The production gate is false in code and
cannot be opened by an imported record flag.

## Implemented boundary

Growth capture records immutable births and accepted transitions, including
before/after dimensions and volume, original zones, then-current morphology
inputs, dissolution, and regrowth. Post-acceptance receipt additions are separate
finalization events. Later edits of old zones are explicit unresolved overprints;
they do not rewrite formation receipts or become geological truth.

Frames preserve completed planes and polygons at their observation step.
Calcite facts outside the supported plain Wulff subset are retained with explicit
surface exclusions. Save/load and the isolated exact-step Record Player consume
this specimen directly; disposable Three.js resources consume only its stored
surface. The shipped recipe saves and shipped renderer remain untouched.

The golden fixture exercises two generations, Mn-bearing material, neighbor
contact, dissolution, and regrowth. It fixes specimen/geometry digests and booked
volumes. Actual Wulff-enabled Elmwood (16 steps, matching the old pilot's explicit
override) is also compared with an uncaptured trajectory; it is not confused with
default Elmwood. The fleet census uses all default authored seed-42 scenarios,
no flag overrides, explicit exclusions, and supported historical steps. CI runs
the focused authority regression through the existing bounded foreman.

The commissioned census completed all 41 scenarios: 44 endpoint calcites, one
supported endpoint, 43 explicit exclusions, and 116 supported historical
crystal-steps. Completed/booked ratios span roughly 14.7–32.7%; there were no
completion errors or observed state mutations. This is deliberately not a
fleet-wide renderer-coverage claim. The standalone recorder also produced frozen
five-frame controlled and sixteen-frame Elmwood examples, reconstructing every
frame before/after save/load with total mesh disposal. Its outputs and proof live
under `.local-evidence/calcite-authority-v1/specimens/` and can be regenerated by
`node pilots/calcite/record.mjs`.

## Follow-up factor audit

The follow-up factor audit (`factor-audit.js`, `factor-report.mjs`, and
`factor-coverage.json`) deepens these existing records without rerunning or
changing growth. It independently reconstructs full, attached, and contacted
volumes from saved planes, detects compensating factor errors, and measures the
full body's a/c/b extents against the booked dimensions. Its bounding ellipsoid
is only an arithmetic comparison reference; it cannot justify a physical rescale
or distinguish a wrong mass model by itself.

Keep coverage sources distinct: the rhombohedral contact/dissolution/regrowth
witnesses are authored fixture material, the supported live Elmwood samples are
scalenohedral growth under an explicit Wulff override, and the default fleet's
116 supported samples cover MVT scalenohedra only. Missing phase/topology fields
in that scalar fleet receipt mean unknown, not an observed absence. The new
report binds to the unchanged fleet receipt and saved specimen hashes; it does
not silently rebake or rebind their provenance.

## Next scientific gate

Do not migrate production based on a deterministic rebuild alone. The next
scientific decision must establish a defensible relationship among conserved
inventory, physical dimensions, Wulff shape, substrate attachment, and contact
boundaries. Changing the inventory law or deriving new physical dimensions would
change the model and requires an explicit choice, model support, and a fresh
fleet drift census. No such change was authorized by the selected fallback.

Face advance/arrest/retreat, surviving internal zone boundaries after dissolution,
and exact inclusion coordinates remain unresolved. Keep them labeled; do not
infer them from final fluid, final habit, or a convenient mesh bound. No
multi-mineral abstraction, graphics polish, Test Quarry architecture, or full
renderer migration belongs in this tranche.
