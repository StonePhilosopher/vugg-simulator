# Dr. Wise hostile-review loop

**Started:** 2026-08-05
**Status:** complete; Round 7 was independently judged satisfied for the defined SIM 242 corrective tranche.

## Reviewer identity and scope

“Dr. Michael Wise” is the name of the adversarial review role requested for this project. In this repository the review is performed by an independent AI reviewer instructed to challenge the game from the perspective of Dr. Michael A. Wise's publicly documented mineralogical expertise. It is not a claim that the Smithsonian scientist personally reviewed or endorsed the game.

The reviewer is kept separate from the implementation pass. It receives the current repository state, tests, research notes, model claims, and rendered/mobile evidence, and is asked to find counterexamples rather than ratify the builder's intent.

## Exit rule

The loop ends only when a fresh hostile review reports **satisfied** and lists no unresolved findings in these classes:

1. correctness or determinism;
2. scientific mechanism, units, conservation, applicability, provenance, or uncertainty;
3. mobile layout, touch/keyboard accessibility, or exact-control reachability;
4. gameplay causality, especially “why did or didn't this form?”;
5. save/replay/model identity or stale review artifacts.

A limitation can close a finding only when it is truthfully bounded, player-visible where relevant, excluded from stronger downstream claims, and recorded as future work. Calling an active but unsupported mechanism “temporary” does not close it.

## Deliberate scientific boundaries entering Round 2

- Pressure-dependent ion-pairing, HKF/SUPCRT aqueous thermodynamics, and boiling are not represented as if solved. Boiling awaits conserved volatile pools.
- Kyanite and sillimanite are reported as phase-field alternatives but do not yet have fabricated nucleation engines.
- Rosasite and aurichalcite literature SI is observer-only until a solid-solution model is validated.
- Apophyllite pressure weighting is an uncertain occurrence inference, not a measured equilibrium boundary.
- Deformation/etch/film directives are visual reconstructions; a future etch engine would need explicit mineral mass removal and returned solute.

These boundaries remain eligible hostile-review findings if the shipped UI, narration, claims, or downstream code contradicts them.
