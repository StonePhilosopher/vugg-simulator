## Scenario notes (author's own rationale)
> The Shigar Valley (Skardu District, Gilgit-Baltistan) hosts hundreds of miarolitic gem pegmatites — mostly sub-horizontal sills, 1-4 m thick — intruding the Dassu orthogneiss dome of the Karakoram Metamorphic Complex, sourced by partial melting of the Dassu felsic gneiss itself (Awais et al. 2022; Agheem et al. 2014, 2015). NOT the Baltoro granite, despite the widespread gem-trade conflation: the mined dikes are startlingly YOUNG — ~7-3 Ma, Pliocene, post-dating Baltoro by >10 Myr (Awais et al. 2022 U-Pb) — coeval with Dassu-dome anatexis and rapid Karakoram exhumation; the workings sit at 3,000-5,000 m in glacial terrain (Blauwet 2006; Smith, Smith & Blauwet 1997). The Dassu / Nyet-Bruk / Yuno / Alchuri cluster is the world's premier source of sharp glassy aquamarine on cleavelandite — the 2019 'King of Kashmir' plate (Goyungo cliff, Nyet-Bruk: >20 deep sky-blue crystals on a ~150 kg albite matrix with smoky quartz; Trinchillo 2020) came from exactly this pocket type, ~30 m into the cliff, in the core zone. Character: Li-poor, Be-F-rich pockets of beryl type — schorl but no elbaite/lepidolite/spodumene, low-alkali beryl (Cs2O 0.03-0.04 wt%), a strong F accessory suite (topaz, green fluorite, fluorapatite). Formal LCT/NYF family assignment is unsettled in the literature (Karim et al. 2026 reads LCT-affine; the pocket mineralogy reads beryl-subtype) — the scenario models the observed mineralogy, not the label.

> Fluid framework — NO Shigar-specific fluid-inclusion microthermometry exists (stated honestly; the Shigar FI record is qualitative: 2- and 3-phase aqueous-carbonic inclusions in aquamarine, G&G 54(1) 2018). Pocket-stage numbers are ANALOG-anchored: gem-pocket crystallization 435-355°C by feldspar-solvus thermometry (London, Hunt & Duval 2020; primary pocket feldspars 431±68°C) and 380±80°C / 1.2±0.7 kbar / ~8 wt% NaCl eq at the Luumäki gem-beryl pocket, the best single analog (Michallik, Wagner & Fusswinkel 2021); miarolitic compilation mean 460±25°C (McCaffrey & Jowitt 2023). This scenario's stage-4 aquamarine event floors T at 430°C, dead inside that window; initial P 1.5 kbar and salinity 8 follow the same analogs. The stage-4 'pocket rupture' framing is literature-real, not flavor: sealed pockets build internal fluid overpressure as crystallization pumps volatiles into the cavity (ΔP up to ~+1.5 kbar modeled at the Oktyabrskaya gem pockets, Peretyazhko et al. 2004; London 2013 'bursting the bubble'), and rupture admits the fresh fluid pulse the event models.

> Anchor mineral: AQUAMARINE — in no scenario's expects_species before this one (the whole beryl family was uncovered; the 76-species census, 2026-07-05). The variety dispatch is held to aquamarine by broth design: Cr+V at trace (no emerald), Mn<2 (no morganite), O2≤0.3 (heliodor's Fe³⁺ gate needs O2>0.5 AND Fe≥15), Fe≥8 at pocket stage (plain beryl/goshenite returns 0 when Fe≥8).

> Cascade discipline: initial Be=8 sits BELOW the beryl family's hard Be≥10 ingredient floor — fully dormant, accumulating, until the stage-4 pocket-rupture pulse delivers the hoarded inventory (~118, Evensen/London/Wallace 1999 beryl-saturation territory) and σ(aquamarine) pins its ~9 ceiling at T≈430°C (inside the family's 350-550°C window). Initial B=5 likewise sits below tourmaline's B≥6 floor so schorl genuinely ARRIVES at stage 2 rather than pre-dating the wall zone. Fe-budget routing (the §4 walk): schorl debits Fe from stage 2 on — the stage-4 event re-floors Fe at 12 (fresh reduced fluid with the rupture) so the blue is guaranteed on Fe-hungry seeds; without it the scenario ships occasional goshenite.

> The hf_etch stage is the Shigar signature — famously etched, sculpted aquamarines (structure-controlled dissolution pits and skeletal etch forms on Pakistani blue beryl: G&G 57(2) Summer 2021 Quarterly Crystal — the late fluid fell out of equilibrium with the very beryl it grew). Mechanism here: late fluid crashes Be (the big crystals took it; σ falls under 1.0) while F>30 and pH<3.0 — exactly _beryl_family_dissolution's etch branch (trigonal pits, stepped faces). grow_quartz's HF note fires on the same chemistry. Smoky quartz is free with composition 'pegmatite': the Rossman [AlO₄]⁰ γ-dose path keys off the radiogenic felsic host. Blue-not-gold: Fe²⁺ in the beryl channels is the blue chromophore (Goldman, Rossman & Parkin 1978); oxidized Fe³⁺ gives heliodor's yellow, which is why the broth pins O2 low.

> Wallrock: 'pegmatite' (inert silicate proxy — pocket pH excursions are unbuffered, which the etch stage needs) + cavity_render 'sharp' + occlusion 0.30, all per the gem_pegmatite precedent (London 2008 pocket photographs: crisp euhedral cavity walls, prisms standing proud). thermal_pulses false — a sealed miarolitic chamber has no fracture-valve hot injections; the seven events ARE the thermal story (600→300°C).
