## variant: twinned

## variant: gwindel

A GWINDEL — a single alpine-fissure quartz crystal whose lattice records a continuous ~{twist}° twist about an a-axis. The twist was incorporated during crystal growth by a screw-dislocation structure; it is neither a stack of rotated individuals nor a shear-deformed crystal.

## variant: sceptre_masking

A MASKING SCEPTRE: a {film_phrase}film frosted the prism faces mid-growth; the termination stayed free and renewed a wider second-generation cap through the film, leaving the masked horizon buried in the stem — the non-corrosive epitaxial-renewal counterpart to the corrosion sceptre.

## variant: sceptre

A SCEPTRE: a fissure seal corroded the gen-1 termination, then a fresh silica breach regenerated a wider second-generation cap over the resorbed tip — the alpine crack-seal habit.

## variant: bent

BENT — the prism is plastically curved: it grew straight, then a later tectonic shear (still-active thrusting) bent the finished crystal, leaving undulose strain. A post-growth overprint, not a growth habit.

## variant: tessin

Tessiner Habitus — steep {h0-il} rhombohedra such as {40-41} and {30-31} dominate over the prism, giving the slender, sharply tapered form.

## variant: growth_oscillation

Growth alternated between rapid pulses (up to {max_rate} µm/step, producing growth hillocks) and slow, high-quality periods near equilibrium. This oscillation would be visible as alternating clear and milky zones.

## variant: morion

Sustained α-bombardment from a uraninite neighbor darkened the lattice to dark smoky — morion in the deepest zones. {rad_count} of {total_count} growth zones record the irradiation.

## variant: smoky

Alpha-damage from nearby uraninite activated Al-hole color centers in {dosed_pct}% of the growth zones — this crystal reads as smoky quartz.

## variant: amethyst_tint

Radiation from adjacent uraninite activated Fe³⁺ color centers — the crystal carries an amethyst tint where dose overlapped Fe-rich zones.

## variant: mild_smoky

A modest α-dose ({avg_rad}) crossed the growth history — the crystal carries a faint smoky tint in the irradiated zones.
